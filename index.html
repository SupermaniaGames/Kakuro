How to play

  • Fill every white square with a digit from 1 to 9.
  • A small number in a black square is a total. The number at the top right is the total for the run of white squares to its right. The number at the bottom left is the total for the run below it.
  • Each run must add up to its total.
  • A digit can not repeat inside one run. The same digit can appear again in a different run.
Totals: 4 and 6 across, 3 and 7 down
Solved. 1+3=4, 2+4=6, 1+2=3, 3+4=7
Allowed: 1+2+3=6
Not allowed: two 3s in one run
  • Some totals can be made only one way. Two squares: 3 is 1+2, 4 is 1+3, 16 is 7+9 and 17 is 8+9. Start with these.
  • Three squares: 6 is 1+2+3, 7 is 1+2+4, 23 is 6+8+9 and 24 is 7+8+9.
  • A square sits in two runs, so it must fit both. If one run can only use 1 and 3, and the other only 1 and 2, the square is 1.
  • Use pencil notes for the digits that are still possible, and rub them out as you learn more.
  • The sums under the board show what the selected runs add up to so far.

Notes: 1 or 3
4 across and 3 down: this square must be 1
  • Tap a white square, then tap a number. The runs that go through the square light up.
  • Notes: switch Notes on to write small candidate digits. Tap a digit again to remove it. When you place a digit, it is cleared from the notes of its runs.
  • Erase clears a square. Undo and Redo walk back and forward through your moves.
  • Mistakes: switch it on to see wrong digits in red. A digit repeated inside a run is always shown in red.
  • Hint: fills in one square. You only get a few per puzzle, and using one counts against your score.
  • On a keyboard: 1 to 9, Backspace, the arrow keys, N for notes and Ctrl+Z to undo.
